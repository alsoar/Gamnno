How to play

For each die, move your own checker forward or your opponent’s checker backward. White advances from point 0 toward 23. Brown advances from 23 toward 0. Select a highlighted source, then its destination.

Landing on a lone checker of the other color sends it to the bar. Moving an opponent’s checker can hit your own. Two or more checkers block the other color. Re-enter your own bar checkers first.

Use as many dice as possible. Doubles give four moves. If only one die can be used, play the higher one if legal.

Once all your checkers reach your half (12–23 for White, 0–11 for Brown), bearing off one wins immediately. Count pips to the tray one step beyond the last point. An exact roll bears off, and an oversized roll can bear off your farthest checker.

Board state

Positive counts are White. Negative counts are Brown. The player is +1 for White and −1 for Brown.
